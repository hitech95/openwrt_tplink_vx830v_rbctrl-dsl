# Software-generated tones in the VX830v ATA

These are the five tone paths generated by the production `rbctl_voip` module.
`voip.board.locale` selects a **board** profile. None of the oscillators below
reads that setting. Frequencies and durations describe the code at 8 kHz, not
measurements of handset loudness or claims of national tone-plan compliance.

| Tone | When it plays | Where the samples are generated | What is generated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audible ringback → calling handset | `sip_event()` selects local ringback on `UA_EVENT_CALL_RINGING` (180); progress/established switches to remote media. This is not the electrical ringing of the called phone. | [`audio.c`, `pull_and_send()`, `l->ringing`](../../rbctl-feed/rbctl-voip/modules/rbctl_voip/audio.c#L108-L115) | 425 Hz, 1 s on / 4 s off, repeating. Fixed Italy-style pattern. |
| Call-waiting beep → busy handset | A second incoming dialog on the occupied line calls `phone_wait_beep(slot, true)`. | [`audio.c`, `phone_wait_beep()`](../../rbctl-feed/rbctl-voip/modules/rbctl_voip/audio.c#L81-L84) sets the length; [`pull_and_send()`](../../rbctl-feed/rbctl-voip/modules/rbctl_voip/audio.c#L120-L121) mixes it into downlink audio. | One 300 ms burst at 425 Hz; existing downlink samples are halved during the mix. Separate from optional board-generated type-II CID. |
| Number unobtainable (special information tone, SIT) → handset | [`call.c`, `progress()`](../../rbctl-feed/rbctl-voip/modules/rbctl_voip/call.c#L218) selects `SIGNAL_UNOBTAINABLE` for the unavailable-number response group. | [`audio.c`, `pull_and_send()`, `sit[]`](../../rbctl-feed/rbctl-voip/modules/rbctl_voip/audio.c#L99-L106) | 950, 1400, then 1800 Hz, 330 ms each, followed by 990 ms silence; repeats until state changes. |
| Off-hook warning (howler) → handset | [`call.c`, `on_timeout()`](../../rbctl-feed/rbctl-voip/modules/rbctl_voip/call.c#L237) selects `SIGNAL_HOWLER` on first-digit or warning timeout, then arms lockout. | [`audio.c`, `pull_and_send()`, `SIGNAL_HOWLER`](../../rbctl-feed/rbctl-voip/modules/rbctl_voip/audio.c#L99-L106) | 1400 Hz, 250 ms on / 250 ms off. `howler_duration` limits total playback, not the waveform. |
| In-band keypad DTMF → SIP peer | In an established call with account `dtmf 'inband'`, handset press/release events set/clear the active digit via `phone_digit()`. | [`audio.c`, `phone_audio_rx()`](../../rbctl-feed/rbctl-voip/modules/rbctl_voip/audio.c#L217-L227) replaces microphone samples before handing them to baresip. | One row frequency (697/770/852/941 Hz) plus one column (1209/1336/1477 Hz) for `0–9`, `*`, `#`. Stops on release, capped at 5 s. RFC 4733 and SIP INFO do not use this oscillator. |

**Board-generated instead:** ordinary dial and stutter dial tones, busy,
congestion, and disconnect use board tone selectors. Actual FXS ringing uses
a board cadence entry. SIP 183 early media comes from the remote endpoint;
the 2100 Hz fax detector listens for a tone rather than generating one. For
board selector measurements and their locale scope, see [peer.md](peer.md#caller-id-ring-and-tone).
